01 / METHOD

Long-horizon continuation
through a two-stage training pipeline.

Short-video supervision does not directly teach the AR teacher to continue from KV-cache states encountered later in long ground-truth videos. LongTake distinguishes this teacher gap from the student gap introduced by replacing ground-truth video with student self-rollout.

01 / TEACHER GAP

Long-Horizon Teacher Forcing

Long-Horizon Teacher Forcing learns continuation from later ground-truth histories with a bounded KV-cache.

02 / STUDENT GAP

Self-rollout DMD

The Long-Horizon AR teacher initializes the student for self-rollout DMD. Optional Hybrid DMD adds conditional supervision over later self-rollout chunks to joint supervision over the initial window.
